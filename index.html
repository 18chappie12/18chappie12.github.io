05 HOW I THINK

Good engineering begins with a better question.

I’m drawn to the overlap of technical clarity and human context. Before adding a feature, I try to understand the problem, strip away noise, and make the next interaction feel obvious.

  1. 01

    Start with the person

    What should feel easier, clearer, or more possible after this is built?

  2. 02

    Build the simple path first

    Establish a dependable core before adding visual or technical complexity.

  3. 03

    Refine what matters

    Use feedback and iteration to improve the moments people actually notice.
